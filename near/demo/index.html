Inspect a NEAR transaction for known attack patterns

Reads the receipt graph, reconciles deposits and refunds, and checks earlier related activity. Calibrated on NEAR Intents; other protocols are marked unsupported.

Sample transactions

    Assessment scale

    High-severity pattern
    A known attack pattern was observed in the receipts. Investigate the evidence.
    Suspicious
    Unusual activity or medium-severity rules need review. Not proof of an attack.
    No known pattern
    Nothing reached an alert threshold in complete, supported data. Not proof of safety.
    Inconclusive
    Receipts are missing or incomplete, so no clean result is possible.
    Unsupported
    Outside NEAR Intents, the detector's calibrated scope.

      Contract source check

      A supporting signal: scores Rust contract source for known bug patterns. Most real bugs are missed.      ShowHide

      Sealed test of v1. The optional v2 was not measurably better on test_v1c, which is not sealed. See near/model/reports/RESULTS.md.

      Detection scope. Analyzes completed transactions; findings support investigation and do not replace a security audit. An 11-category taxonomy describes attack types; served rules cover 3: callback accounting, access control and resource exhaustion. Token authenticity and wallet drainers are not covered. Calibrated only on NEAR Intents; other protocols are unsupported. Incomplete graphs can hide evidence. No known pattern does not mean safe.
