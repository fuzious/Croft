Enforcement

A compromised agent can skip the wrapper. It cannot skip its key's permissions or the router.

  1. Oracle wrapper

    Refuses on block, asks a human on warn, and checks the receiver before signing.

  2. Scoped function-call key

    Can call router.pay and nothing else: no transfers, deposits or new keys.

  3. Guarded router

    Pays only with a fresh on-chain allow, within the funded balance and daily limit.

Prompt-injection demo: 12 / 12 checks pass. A scripted agent follows a hidden instruction in an invoice; a confirmed report blocks the receiver. Real contracts, local sandbox, no live LLM.
